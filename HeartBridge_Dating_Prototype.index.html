Safety first 🛡️

HeartBridge is designed as an adults-only (18+) dating concept. For a real launch, we would need age controls, reporting and blocking, moderation, privacy controls, secure authentication, and clear consent-based messaging.

  • Never send money or identity documents to strangers.
  • Keep your exact address and phone number private.
  • Meet first in a public place and tell someone you trust.
  • Report harassment, scams or impersonation.
